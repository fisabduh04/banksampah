INSTALLATION

1. Keep your existing Laravel Boost AGENTS.md unchanged.
2. Copy the .ai folder from this package into the root of your Laravel project.
3. Final structure should look like:

project-root/
├── AGENTS.md
├── artisan
├── composer.json
└── .ai/
    └── rules/
        ├── index.md
        ├── project-context.md
        ├── learning-guidelines.md
        ├── architecture.md
        ├── database.md
        ├── financial-integrity.md
        ├── inventory.md
        ├── filament-ui.md
        ├── attachments.md
        └── testing-production.md

Do not replace the Laravel Boost AGENTS.md with these files.
